# Product Hunt Launch Guide for LYCHO

## Pre-Launch Preparation

### 1. Create Product Hunt Account
- Sign up at producthunt.com
- Complete your profile with LYCHO branding
- Follow relevant categories (AI, SaaS, Developer Tools)

### 2. Prepare Assets
- **Logo:** 240x240px PNG, transparent background
- **Thumbnail:** 1200x628px (OG image format)
- **Gallery Images:** 4-6 screenshots showing:
  - Dashboard Command Center
  - Agent marketplace (370+ agents)
  - WhatsApp integration
  - Analytics dashboard
  - Mobile app
- **Tagline:** "AI agents that run your business 24/7"
- **Description:** Focus on Pakistani market + global expansion

### 3. First Comment
Write a compelling maker comment that tells the story:
- Why you built LYCHO
- The problem it solves for Pakistani businesses
- Technical highlights (7 core agents, 20 sectors, multi-channel)
- What makes it different from competitors

### 4. Launch Day Checklist
- [ ] Submit at 12:01 AM PST (start of the day)
- [ ] Share on personal social media immediately
- [ ] Ask team/friends to upvote and comment
- [ ] Respond to every comment within 30 minutes
- [ ] Monitor ranking throughout the day
- [ ] Share milestone updates (#1, #2, etc.)

### 5. Post-Launch
- Thank everyone who supported
- Share metrics and learnings
- Follow up with interested users
- Collect email addresses for waitlist

## LYCHO-Specific Talking Points

### Unique Value Propositions
1. **370+ specialist agents** across 20 business sectors
2. **Multi-channel:** WhatsApp, Telegram, Email, Web Widget, SMS, Slack, Instagram, Facebook
3. **Built for emerging markets** — PKR pricing, local payment methods
4. **ORION intelligence layer** — geo-aware, culturally intelligent AI
5. **Syndicate network** — agents collaborate and share intelligence
6. **Forge Agent** — autonomous nightly agent creation
7. **Nexus automation engine** — no-code workflow builder

### Target Audience
- Small and medium businesses in Pakistan
- Global businesses looking for affordable AI automation
- Developers building on top of AI agent platforms

### Competitive Advantages
- First AI agent platform built specifically for Pakistani market
- Comprehensive sector coverage (healthcare, legal, real estate, etc.)
- Multi-language support (Urdu, Arabic, English auto-detection)
- Affordable pricing starting at PKR 9,900/month
